## Decisions already made (don't relitigate without reason)

- **Universe:** 11 tickers in 4 sectors: banks JPM, MS; tech GOOGL, MSFT, NVDA; energy XOM, CVX, COP; consumer WMT, PG, KO. BAC, WFC, C, GS, PNC, USB excluded: edgartools 10-Q MD&A extraction fails silently or returns the wrong section for them (details in notebook 01).
- **Section:** MD&A only (10-Q Part I Item 2 = `part_i_item_2`; 10-K Part II Item 7 = `part_ii_item_7`). Risk Factors YoY from 10-Ks is backlog.
- **Window:** last 6 10-Qs + 2 10-Ks per ticker (= 8 quarters; no Q4 10-Q). This gives only 3 YoY pairs per ticker; raising `n_10q` to ~9 is under consideration.
- **Pairing rule** (`src/pairs.py`), on fiscal year/quarter, never calendar dates:
  - `yoy`: 10-Q vs same fiscal quarter last year (primary; removes seasonal wording)
  - `qoq`: 10-Q vs previous 10-Q in the same fiscal year (Q1 has none)
  - `annual`: 10-K vs prior 10-K (10-K MD&A is structured too differently to compare with a 10-Q)
  - amendments excluded from pairs
- **Fiscal quarters** (`fiscal_quarter()` in ingest.py): derived from SEC's `fiscal_year_end` (MMDD). Fiscal year named for the calendar year it ends in (MSFT quarter ending 2025-09-30 = FY2026 Q1). Dates in the first 7 days of a month count as the previous month, for 52/53-week calendars (KO Q2 2026 ended 2026-07-03).
- **Acceptance time:** from `filing.header.acceptance_datetime` (SGML header, US Eastern), not `filing.acceptance_datetime` from the filings list, which edgartools documents as sometimes hours off. Accepted at/after 16:00 ET -> event day is the next trading day (`accepted_after_close` in the manifest).
- **Diffing:** order-independent paragraph matching. difflib's positional diff fails on moved / lightly edited paragraphs (notebook 02). Mask numbers, %, $ and date phrases before comparing so number-only updates are separated from real language changes.
- **Event study:** market model vs SPY and sector ETF (XLF, XLK, XLC, XLE, XLP), estimation window -250 to -30 trading days, CARs [0,+1], [-1,+1], [0,+5]. Caveat: 10-Qs usually follow the earnings release, so separate the two (record earnings dates).

## Known data quirks

- MD&A word counts range ~4.4k (NVDA 10-Q) to ~64k (JPM 10-K).
- Page junk differs by company: MSFT "PART I"/"Item 2"; GOOGL "Table of Contents  Alphabet Inc."; JPM/MS/PG/COP footers with the page number inside. clean.py only drops repeated short lines *near a page break*: dropping every repeated short line (first try) removed real segment headings and sentences that differ only in numbers.
- Page-split joins only when clearly mid-sentence (second half starts lowercase, or first half's last word is lowercase / ends in a comma, and second starts with a letter): joining any two text blocks glued tables, bullets and footnotes to neighbours (JPM had 807 "joins").
- XOM 2026 10-Qs (2026-03-31, 2026-06-30) are hard-wrapped by edgartools: blank line after every printed line. The join rule fixes them (130-150 joins each, vs 0-2 for other XOM filings); median paragraph length now in line.
- CVX page header is 15 words, so REPEAT_MAX_WORDS is 20, not 12.
- Known clean.py misses: a few page-top headings dropped (JPM "Quarterly results", XOM "Volume / Mix", KO/PG "Nine months ended ... versus ..."); header-heavy tables tagged text; chart labels sometimes joined to the next paragraph.
- Check per-filing join counts and median paragraph length after any clean.py change: an outlier filing (like XOM 2026) hides in ticker totals.
- MD&A length seems to grow through the fiscal year (Q1 shortest): check if real before normalizing.
- `filing_date` is the next business day for filings accepted after 17:30 ET (all 8 GOOGL, 1 PG): never use it as the event date.
- Accepted after close for all 8 filings: MSFT, NVDA, GOOGL, JPM, MS, PG, WMT. COP, CVX, KO always intraday; XOM 2 of 8 after close. After-close does not mean "with earnings": JPM, MS, WMT file 2-4 weeks after results. Not yet checked against actual earnings dates.
- Read the manifest with `dtype={"fiscal_year_end": str}`, or "0630" loads as 630.
- CVX, JPM, XOM 10-K Item 7 runs into Item 8 (management's report, auditor's report; 4-22% of words). Validation doesn't catch it; clean.py cuts at the first whole-line Item 8 heading. XOM 2024 10-K lists those headings in a table of contents (line 15, with page numbers), so never cut on a plain substring match.
- `word_count` in the manifest is on raw text (includes that overrun).
- 10-K vs 10-Q MD&A length: ~1x for MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA; 1.3-2.2x for most; 3.9x for XOM.
